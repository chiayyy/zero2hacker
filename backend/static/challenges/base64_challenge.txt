===============================================
       BASE64 ENCODING CHALLENGE
===============================================

Base64 is used everywhere in computing!
It's an encoding scheme - NOT encryption.

Your task: Decode this Base64 string to find the flag.

ENCODED MESSAGE:
-------------------
RkxBR3tiYXNlNjRfZGVjb2Rpbmdfcm9ja3N9
-------------------

TOOLS YOU CAN USE:
- Online: base64decode.org
- Linux/Mac: echo "string" | base64 -d
- Python: import base64; print(base64.b64decode("string").decode())
- Windows PowerShell: [System.Text.Encoding]::UTF8.GetString([System.Convert]::FromBase64String("string"))
