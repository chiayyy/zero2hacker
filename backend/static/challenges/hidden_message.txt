===============================================
    STEGANOGRAPHY - HIDDEN MESSAGE
===============================================

Sometimes secrets hide in plain sight...
The art of hiding messages is called steganography.
Look carefully at the poem below.

-------------------
THE HIDDEN POEM
-------------------

Softly whispers the wind at night,
Telling tales of ancient might.
Every shadow holds a key,
Gently waiting, wild and free.

Always searching, never still,
Near the mountains, past the hill.
Over rivers, through the glen,
Great adventures start and then...

Rivers flow to distant seas,
Amazing views through autumn trees.
Perhaps you've found what hides in plain,
Hidden meanings in the rain.
Years of secrets, now revealed!

-------------------

HINT: Read the FIRST LETTER of each line in the poem above.
The flag format is: FLAG{first_word_second_word}

Example: If letters spell "HELLOWORLD", flag might be FLAG{hello_world}
